# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This feature involves redesigning the team page to maintain visual consistency with the home page's light blue color scheme while updating the content to showcase the two founders with enhanced visual appeal and messaging that emphasizes the student-led nature of the organization.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a visitor to the website, I want to see a consistent light blue color scheme on the team page that matches the home page, so that the site feels cohesive and professional.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user visits the team page THEN the page SHALL use the same light blue color palette as the home page (#E6F2FF, #B3D9E5, #2C4D6E, #1A6B8A)
2. WHEN the page loads THEN the background SHALL feature gradient styling consistent with other pages
3. WHEN comparing to the home page THEN the color scheme SHALL be visually harmonious

### Requirement 2

**User Story:** As a visitor to the website, I want to see compelling messaging about the student-led nature of the organization at the top of the team page, so that I understand the unique perspective and mission of EFOF.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user visits the team page THEN the hero section SHALL display "A team of students building and bringing you the future of education" as the main heading
2. WHEN viewing the hero section THEN the subtext SHALL read "Education for Our Futures is entirely student-led, principally driven by those who truly live in the education system. With fresh perspectives and bold ideas, we're reimagining what learning can look like by bringing the right voices to the table and turning vision into action."
3. WHEN the page loads THEN the messaging SHALL be prominently displayed at the top of the page

### Requirement 3

**User Story:** As a visitor to the website, I want to see exactly two team members (the founders) displayed prominently, so that I can learn about the key leadership of the organization.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user views the team section THEN exactly two team members SHALL be displayed
2. WHEN viewing the team members THEN Rishal Melvani SHALL be shown with the title "Founder"
3. WHEN viewing the team members THEN Ishaan Singh SHALL be shown with the title "Founder"
4. WHEN the team section loads THEN both founders SHALL be presented with equal prominence

### Requirement 4

**User Story:** As a visitor to the website, I want the team member display to be visually appealing and creative, so that the page feels engaging and professional.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN viewing the team members THEN the layout SHALL be visually appealing with creative design elements
2. WHEN the page loads THEN the team member cards SHALL have enhanced styling compared to the current basic layout
3. WHEN viewing on different screen sizes THEN the layout SHALL be responsive and maintain visual appeal
4. WHEN hovering over team member cards THEN there SHALL be subtle interactive effects to enhance user experience
